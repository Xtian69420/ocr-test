Image to Text AI

Convert an Image to Text

This process involves extracting readable and editable text from an image using Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology. It allows users to scan documents, photos, screenshots, or handwritten notes and convert the visual content into digital text that can be copied, edited, or searched. This is useful for digitizing printed material, automating data entry, and enhancing accessibility.

GCash Payment Transaction Extractor

A lightweight tool designed to automatically identify and extract reference numbers from GCash transaction messages or receipts. Whether you're processing SMS notifications, email confirmations, or payment screenshots, this extractor streamlines the process by isolating the unique transaction ID—saving time, reducing errors, and ensuring accurate records for personal tracking, business reconciliations, or app integrations.
